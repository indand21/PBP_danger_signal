## The figure

Figure 2A, *P. aeruginosa* ATCC-27853, measured MIC 1 mg/L.

Downloaded 2026-10-14:

- Web image: https://www.frontiersin.org/files/Articles/840692/xml-images/fphar-13-840692-g002.webp
- Publisher PDF: https://public-pages-files-2025.frontiersin.org/journals/pharmacology/articles/10.3389/fphar.2022.840692/pdf

The PDF is excluded by the repository's existing `*.pdf` rule. The extracted
page-4 image, `figure2_embedded0.png` (1978 x 1276 pixels), is the coordinate
reference for `../meropenem_figure2a_multiarm.csv`. The web image provides an
independent rendering of the same figure, not an independent experiment.

Calibration in the extracted image: log10 CFU/mL 10 at y approximately 26;
log10 CFU/mL 1 at y approximately 318. Thus
`log10_CFU = 1 + (318 - pixel_y) * 9 / 292`.
Time 0 is x approximately 131, 2 h x approximately 198, 9.6 h x approximately
451. Values are rounded to 0.1 log10; pixel locations are approximate marker
centres, not high-precision measurements. See the annotated source overlay
generated by `scripts/ds_multiarm_refit.py` for review.

Only t=0, 2, 9.6 h are considered: drug was removed at 9.6 h. The 24 h points
are outside the constant-exposure experiment and excluded.

Legend: square 16x, up triangle 8x, down triangle 4x, diamond 2x, open star 1x,
left triangle 0.5x, right triangle 0.25x, open circle native growth control,
filled circle centrifuged growth control.

Seven concentration arms exist, but the 0.5x markers cannot be separated
confidently from the growth-control cluster in these raster renderings.
Their values are deliberately missing, not zero, and excluded from the
primary fit. Sensitivity scenarios place them at the native/centrifuged
control values; these are explicitly inferred scenarios, not new data.
The marker assignment to that cluster is itself uncertain: these scenarios
test a possible reading and do not establish the location of the hidden
left-triangle markers.
The star/down-triangle at 2 h and the high-dose cluster at 9.6 h also overlap;
these readings carry quality flags and are tested with bounded perturbations.
The reading error allowance of +/-0.3 log10 is inherited from the older
digitisation note. It is not a measured SD or a confidence interval.

The baseline markers overlap. The analysis uses one common baseline of
4.8 log10 with that same reading allowance, rather than counting nine
repeated baseline readings as independent observations. Both growth-control
curves are included, with equal point weights. Published curves are triplicate
summaries; no replicate-level measurements or sampling-error estimates have
been recovered.

The earlier `../meropenem_timekill.csv` is retained for reproducibility. Its
4x values (5.0, 4.6, 3.0) differ from this re-reading (4.8, 4.4, 2.9) within
the stated reading allowance. Do not count the old and new 4x readings as
independent data, or relabel these calibration arms as held-out validation.
